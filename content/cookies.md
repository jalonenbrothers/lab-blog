# Cookie Policy

Last updated: [PLACEHOLDER: date]

## The short version

This site sets **one** cookie, only when you sign in, and only for
keeping you signed in. We run **no analytics, tracking, or
advertising cookies** at present. Reading the blog never sets any
cookie. Your "OK" on the cookie banner is stored in your browser's
local storage, not a cookie.

## The cookie we set

| Cookie | Type | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PHPSESSID` (session) | Strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in after Google Sign-In and protects the form you use against cross-site request issues | Session (deleted when you close your browser) |

## Local storage

| Key | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ck-ok` | Remembers you dismissed the cookie banner (value: "1") |
| `theme` | Your dark/light theme choice |

## Analytics — currently none

We do not use any analytics at present. If we ever add analytics,
we will update this policy **before** enabling it, and (because we
are EU/UK oriented) we will only use a tool configuration that does
not require consent by default (e.g. cookieless, IP-truncating) or
we will ask for consent first.

## Third parties

The sign-in button loads Google's Identity Services script
(`accounts.google.com/gsi/client`) only on pages that contain a
comment/spark area. Google may set its own cookies when you click
the button; those are governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
If you never sign in, nothing is loaded from Google.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site
remains fully readable. Blocking the session cookie only disables
sign-in, sparks, and commenting.
