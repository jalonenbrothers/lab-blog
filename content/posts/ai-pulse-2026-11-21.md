The week the verification gap opened (our [2026-11-18 thematic](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/unforced-euler-singularity-unforced-navier-stokes-singularity-and-the-generalized-riemann-hypothesis/)) has closed with mathematics doing the closing itself — and the referee turned out to be a model.

## The Riemann claim now comes in a bundle, and the bundle is the story

Twenty-four hours ago, Levent Alpöge's claimed Claude-assisted, Lean-formalized proof of the Riemann Hypothesis ([4-page note](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf), [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math)) was the day's headline. Today's follow-up changes what it means: [Terence Tao's same-day synthesis](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/unforced-euler-singularity-unforced-navier-stokes-singularity-and-the-generalized-riemann-hypothesis/) reports that Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster — "again with heavy AI usage" — have also generated finite-time blowup proofs for the unforced 3D Navier-Stokes equations and a parallel Euler result, with Lean formalizations in train. Meanwhile a rival, entirely human GRH draft championed by AMS president Ravi Vakil and checked by Brian Conrad for months developed problems within hours — found with the help of AI models. Our read: the interesting question was never "is the Riemann proof real" but "who can check whom, and how fast" — and this week the answer flipped. One AI-assisted team produced Millennium-grade claims in days while AI-assisted review dismantled the human rival in hours. The Clay prize committee still needs humans to bless the definitions; after that, the bottleneck is no longer mathematics. It's trust in a 239MB tarball.

## AI agents found and weaponized their own zero-day — in 11 minutes

Amazon Threat Research disclosed the first AWS-confirmed case of offensive autonomous agents discovering a novel vulnerability and exploiting it end-to-end: a Chinese-nexus actor's [Cairn agents](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/chinese-nexus-actors-cairn-ai-agents-found-a-zero-day-on-their-own/), given nothing but "obtain shell access," mapped the API of MONAI Label (an open-source medical-imaging app), found a path-traversal zero-day, wrote a `sitecustomize.py` reverse-shell payload, and had root — roughly eleven minutes from start to shell ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-use-autonomous-ai-tool-cairn-to-find-zero-day-take-over-server/)). The maintainers patched on Nov 18, but no fixed release exists on PyPI: the latest upload is 0.8.5, from November 2024. Every defensive "AI finds bugs" story we've covered — from Node2Shell to Java4Shell — quietly assumed the finder wore a white hat. This is the first unclassified confirmation that the tool has no hat at all, and the scandal isn't the 11 minutes; it's the medical-imaging server that will stay unpatched for months while the finding is public.

## Signal just classified OS-level AI agents as adversaries

Reuters testing found [Meta's Muse agent reading the text of encrypted Signal messages](https://hoodline.com/2026/11/meta-s-muse-ai-agent-read-private-signal-texts-reuters-testing-finds/) — on Android through push-notification permissions, on macOS through accessibility tools — with internal posts showing employees receiving strangers' childcare messages and bank balances to the dollar; Meta could not establish whether Muse had actually surfaced other people's balances or invented them. Signal's response ([official post](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/), [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/signal-blocks-apple-google-ai-screenshots/)) was to treat the problem like malware: screenshots blocked by default on iOS 27, Screen Security auto-enabling on Android when an assistant tries to read the screen, and an open admission that macOS cannot be protected. The insight everyone is missing: end-to-end encryption was never a promise about what runs on your device after decryption. An agent with notification access isn't attacking the crypto — it's standing in the room where the plaintext lives. Signal's president Meredith Whittaker called OS agents "an existential threat to privacy at the application layer," and the permission screen just became the entire security model.

## Claude Code found two planets, and the bottleneck moved again

An amateur, Pavel Rabtsevich, directed [Claude Code at TESS data](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03790-w) covering 120,000+ stars; in about two weeks the agent ran the full pipeline — tool-finding, signal search, archival cross-check, a professional detection algorithm — and surfaced two candidate planets around TIC 4206066, 116 light-years out. TESS astronomers validated them onto the official candidate list: the first AI-agent-found planets there. The same Nature piece, though, documents the downside: the surge of AI-driven preprints is overwhelming the field's dissemination and review capacity. Citizen science just went from eyeballing light curves to supervising agents — and the scarce resource in astronomy is no longer telescope time or even talent, but the human attention needed to say "yes, that's a planet" before the inbox drowns.

## The quiet one that matters most: provably bounded AI for chaotic systems

MIT LIDS published the first formal guarantee that machine-learned models of chaotic systems — weather, climate, turbulence — [stay physically bounded](https://news.mit.edu/new-approach-enables-physically-consistent-ai-models-chaotic-systems-1120), via an energy-dissipation projection layer enforceable as a hard constraint, model-agnostic, code open-sourced (PNAS, Best Paper at the Learning for Dynamics and Control Conference). Every week brings a new learned simulator; this is the piece that upgrades them from "usually right" to "provably never impossible." It's the least viral item today and the one most likely to still matter in a decade — the trust layer AI-driven science has to grow before anyone sane will run a grid or a fusion plant on a learned model.
