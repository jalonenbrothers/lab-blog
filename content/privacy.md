## What we collect

**Account data (only if you sign in with Google).** When you use the
"Sign in with Google" option, we receive your Google account
identifier, display name, and email address. We store:

- your display name (as it appears next to your comments; you can
  ask us to change it),
- a one-way hashed (salted SHA-256) copy of your email address —
  used only to look up your account; the readable email is **not**
  stored,
- Google's account identifier (needed to recognise you on return).

**Content you submit.** Comments and "Sparks" ⚡ you give a post.
Comments are held in a moderation queue and are not published until
approved by a human moderator. URLs and email addresses inside
comments are automatically masked before storage and are **never**
unmasked or published.

**Technical basics.** A server-side session cookie (see the Cookie
Policy) when you sign in. We run Google Analytics **only with your explicit consent**: the
analytics script is not loaded unless you click Accept on the cookie
banner. Declining is one click, loads nothing, and hides no content —
the full site works identically either way. GA processes visit data
at aggregated, IP-anonymized level for audience measurement only; no
advertising, no cross-site tracking, no profiling. See the
[cookie policy](/cookies) for the cookie names and how to change
your mind.

## Why we process it (legal bases, GDPR/UK GDPR)

- Sign-in, sparks, and comments: **legitimate interest** in running
  an interactive blog, and **performance of a service** you request
  (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)). You can exercise your rights below at any time.
- Moderation and abuse prevention: **legitimate interest** in
  keeping the site spam-free and lawful.
- No special-category data is processed. No profiling. No automated
  decision-making. No sale or sharing of personal data — ever.

## Retention

- Unapproved/deleted comments: removed within 30 days.
- Approved comments: kept until you ask us to remove them or the
  post is deleted.
- Account records: kept until you request deletion.
- Moderation logs: 12 months, then purged.
